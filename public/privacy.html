Important Notice on Google User Data: GoldMailer respects user privacy and enforces strict limitations on access and use of information received through Google APIs. 1. What is GoldMailer?

GoldMailer (accessible at https://goldmailer.xyz) is a permanent, secure email and unified communications platform designed to provide individuals and organizations with permanent webmail accounts (under the @goldmailer.xyz domain), generous 15GB cloud storage, military-grade two-step verification, integrated SMS communication tools, and a unified inbox that aggregates communications in one clean, fast, and respectful environment.

Unlike disposable or ephemeral email services, GoldMailer is a full-featured, persistent email suite that values long-term user ownership, complete data isolation, and user privacy.

2. What Gmail Data We Collect via Google OAuth

When you voluntarily choose to connect your external Google/Gmail account to GoldMailer using Google OAuth 2.0 (located under Settings > Connected Emails), we request the minimum necessary permissions to provide auto-forwarding and inbox synchronization services. Specifically, we collect and process the following Google user data:

  • Google Account Email Address: We collect your Gmail email address to identify your account and verify that you are the legitimate owner of the connected email.
  • Email Subject Lines: Used to display message subjects in your GoldMailer inbox and search results.
  • Email Senders & Recipients: The "From", "To", "Cc", and "Bcc" header addresses to accurately reflect message threads and contact names.
  • Email Body Content (Text & HTML): The plain text and HTML content of incoming emails so you can read, review, and organize your messages within the GoldMailer interface.
  • Message Timestamps & Message IDs: Delivery timestamps and unique RFC/Google message identifiers used to sort your inbox chronologically and prevent duplicate imports.
  • OAuth Access & Refresh Tokens: Securely stored programmatic tokens issued by Google that permit GoldMailer to synchronize messages on your behalf.

The specific Google OAuth scopes requested by GoldMailer are:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly — To read and fetch incoming emails and their metadata into your GoldMailer inbox.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basic — To optionally configure native auto-forwarding to your GoldMailer forwarding address.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — To view your Google email address for account mapping and verification.

3. Why We Collect Gmail Data (Purpose of Processing)

GoldMailer collects and processes your Gmail data solely for the following explicit purposes:

  • Displaying Emails in Your Inbox: To present, format, and render your incoming messages within the unified GoldMailer webmail client with a clear "Forwarded from: example@gmail.com" label.
  • Inbox Search and Organization: To allow you to search, categorize, star, and sort messages across all your connected email addresses in one place.
  • Automatic Forwarding & Syncing: To poll and retrieve newly arrived messages in your Gmail account every few minutes or on demand when you click "Sync Now".

We do NOT use Gmail data for any other purpose. Specifically, we do not use your Gmail content or metadata for personalized advertising, behavioral profiling, market research, or training commercial artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data (Encryption & Security)

We maintain rigorous technical and administrative security measures to safeguard all user information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between your browser, our servers, and Google APIs is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Gmail tokens and email records are stored in hardened database instances protected by industry-standard encryption protocols. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are hashed using bcrypt with dynamic salts.
  • Account Isolation: Each GoldMailer user account is logically isolated. No other user can access, read, or query your connected Gmail data or messages.
  • Restricted System Access: Access to production servers and database environments is restricted strictly to authorized administrative personnel through multi-factor authentication.

5. Absolute Prohibition on Selling or Sharing Gmail Data

Zero Data Selling Commitment: GoldMailer does NOT sell, rent, trade, monetize, or share your Gmail data, contact details, or Google account information to any third parties, advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks under any circumstances.

Your data is never transferred to third parties, except in the following strictly limited legal circumstances:

7. User Control, Data Deletion, and Revoking Access

You have complete control over your connected Gmail accounts and all associated data at all times:

A. Disconnecting Inside GoldMailer

You can disconnect your Gmail account at any time directly from the GoldMailer user interface:

  1. Log into GoldMailer at https://goldmailer.xyz.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon) and select the Connected Emails tab.
  3. Click the Delete / Disconnect (trash icon) button next to your connected account.

Upon disconnection, GoldMailer immediately deletes your stored Google OAuth access and refresh tokens from our database, halting any future synchronization. You can also empty your Trash folder to permanently purge all previously imported messages.

B. Revoking Access Directly via Google

You can independently revoke GoldMailer's access to your Google Account at any time directly from Google's security dashboard:

Visit: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Locate GoldMailer under third-party apps with account access, and click Remove Access. Google will instantly invalidate all issued tokens, permanently severing all connectivity.
